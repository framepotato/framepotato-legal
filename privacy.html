Privacy Policy for Framepotato Analytics Sync Last Updated: September 12, 2026 1. Overview Framepotato Analytics Sync ("we", "our", or "us") is a private internal analytical tool developed to monitor and organize video performance metrics for the @framepotato TikTok account within a personal Google Sheet. 2. Data We Collect When authorized via TikTok OAuth, our application accesses public account and video data strictly made available by the TikTok API, including: Public profile details (display name, handle) Public video attributes (video ID, title, publication date, duration) Aggregate performance metrics (view count, like count, comment count, share count) 3. How We Use Your Data Collected data is used exclusively to populate internal reporting tables and performance dashboards inside the account owner's private Google Sheet. We do not sell, distribute, monetize, or process this data for external marketing, tracking, or profiling purposes. 4. Data Storage and Security All retrieved video metrics are transmitted directly through official TikTok API endpoints (open.tiktokapis.com) to Google Apps Script. Data is stored solely within the designated Google Spreadsheet owned by the account administrator. No external third-party servers or databases store or retain your authentication tokens or video metrics. 5. Data Retention & Access Revocation OAuth access tokens are temporarily held in Google Apps Script UserProperties solely to authenticate API requests. You can revoke this application's access at any time by navigating to your TikTok Account Settings under Security and permissions > Apps and sessions and removing permissions for Framepotato Analytics Sync. 6. Contact Us For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the tool's data processing, please contact the account owner directly at your associated email address.
